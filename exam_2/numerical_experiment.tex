\section{Numerical experiment}
\subsection{LMS experimentation}
Looking at the equations for the LMS filter, we're given some handles to tune the filter with
namely $\mu$, $M$ the amount of filter taps and $N$ how long the algorithm will run.

For the initial tests, we'll keep the amount of filter tabs fixed to $M=3$, and we'll instead 
vary the step size by a factor of 1000, and keep the test running for a 2000 samples. This is an
estimate to have enought samples to atleast at higher values for $\mu$ that the filter will converge.

The next experiment is increasing the filter taps, where $M = [3,5,10]$. This is done to observe
if increasing the filter tabs, beyond the amount of poles present in the unidentified system, has 
any influence on either convergence speed and accuracy.
